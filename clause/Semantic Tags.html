Biography of MS Dhoni

Early Life

Mahendra Singh Dhoni was born in Ranchi, Jharkhand on 7 July 1981. He was the youngest child of Pan Singh and Devaki Devi. During his school days at DAV Jawahar Vidya Mandir, he showed interest in football and badminton before moving towards cricket.

Career in Cricket

Dhoni officially started his cricket career with the Central Coalfields Limited team in 1998. His performances helped him enter the Bihar cricket team.

Ranji Trophy

Dhoni made his Ranji Trophy debut during the 1999–2000 season at the age of 18.

India-A Team

Dhoni toured Kenya and Zimbabwe and impressed everyone with his batting and wicketkeeping.

Captain of the Indian Team

Dhoni became captain of the Indian team in 2007 and won many ICC trophies.

Indian Premier League

Dhoni became the captain of Chennai Super Kings and won multiple IPL titles.

Awards and Honours

MS Dhoni received Padma Bhushan, Padma Shri and Major Dhyan Chand Khel Ratna awards.
